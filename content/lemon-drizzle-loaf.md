The trick to a drizzle cake is that the drizzle is not icing. It is lemon juice and granulated sugar poured over a hot cake, so the juice sinks in and the sugar stays on top as a thin, crackling crust. Get that distinction right and everything else follows.

## Ingredients

**For the loaf**

- 200g unsalted butter, softened
- 200g caster sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 200g self-raising flour
- Zest of 3 unwaxed lemons
- 3 tbsp whole milk
- Pinch of salt

**For the drizzle**

- Juice of 2 lemons (about 80ml)
- 100g granulated sugar — not caster, you want the crunch

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line a 900g loaf tin, leaving an overhang to lift the cake out later.
2. Cream the butter and caster sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
3. Fold in the flour, salt and lemon zest, then loosen with the milk.
4. Scrape into the tin and bake for 45–50 minutes, until deep golden and a skewer comes out clean. If it colours too fast, lay a sheet of foil over the top for the last 15 minutes.
5. Stir the lemon juice and granulated sugar together — **do not let the sugar dissolve**.
6. While the cake is still hot and in its tin, prick it all over with a skewer and spoon the drizzle slowly over the surface. Let it sink in fully before lifting the cake out.

## Tips

- Zest the lemons before you juice them. Everyone learns this once.
- Rub the zest into the sugar with your fingertips first if you want a more pronounced lemon flavour — it releases the oils.
- Keeps for four days wrapped, and improves on the second.
