Its reputation was ruined by decades of frozen supermarket versions with synthetic cream and glacé cherries. Made properly — with morello cherries, real kirsch and cream barely sweetened at all — it is a genuinely serious cake.

## Ingredients

**For the sponge**

- 6 large eggs, separated
- 175g caster sugar
- 100g plain flour
- 50g cocoa powder
- 75g unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- Pinch of salt

**For the filling**

- 700g jar of morello cherries in syrup, drained (reserve the syrup)
- 4 tbsp kirsch
- 600ml double cream, very cold
- 2 tbsp icing sugar
- 100g dark chocolate, for shaving

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line a deep 23cm tin.
2. Whisk the yolks with two-thirds of the sugar until thick and pale enough to leave a ribbon trail.
3. In a spotlessly clean bowl, whisk the whites with the salt to soft peaks, then add the remaining sugar in a stream and whisk to firm, glossy peaks.
4. Sift the flour and cocoa over the yolk mixture and fold gently. Fold in a third of the whites to loosen, then the rest in two additions. Finally fold in the melted butter.
5. Bake 30–35 minutes until the cake springs back. Cool completely, then slice horizontally into three layers.

## Assembly

1. Mix 100ml of the reserved cherry syrup with the kirsch. Brush generously over all three layers and let it soak in — the sponge should be damp, not merely flavoured.
2. Whip the cream with the icing sugar to soft peaks. Stop early; it will firm as you spread it.
3. Layer sponge, cream, cherries, sponge, cream, cherries, sponge. Cover the top and sides with the remaining cream.
4. Shave the chocolate with a vegetable peeler and press it around the sides. Finish with cherries on top.

## Tips

- Morello cherries, not glacé. The sourness is the entire point of the cake.
- Assemble at least four hours ahead so the layers settle and cut cleanly.
- Leave the kirsch out and it becomes a chocolate cherry cake — pleasant, but something else entirely.
