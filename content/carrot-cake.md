An oil cake rather than a butter cake, which is why it stays damp for days while a sponge goes stale overnight. The carrot contributes moisture and very little flavour — the spices do the work.

## Ingredients

**For the cake**

- 250ml sunflower oil
- 250g light brown soft sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 250g plain flour
- 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- ½ tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- ½ tsp fine salt
- 300g carrots, coarsely grated (about 4 medium)
- 100g walnuts, roughly chopped
- 75g sultanas
- Zest of 1 orange

**For the frosting**

- 300g full-fat cream cheese, cold
- 100g unsalted butter, softened
- 100g icing sugar, sifted
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line two 20cm tins.
2. Whisk the oil, sugar and eggs together until smooth and slightly thickened.
3. Sift in the flour, bicarbonate, spices and salt and fold until barely combined.
4. Fold through the carrot, walnuts, sultanas and orange zest. The batter will be looser than you expect.
5. Divide between the tins and bake 30–35 minutes, until springy and a skewer comes out clean.
6. Cool completely. Frosting a warm carrot cake produces soup.

## The frosting

Beat the butter and icing sugar until smooth, then add the **cold** cream cheese and vanilla and beat only until it comes together — thirty seconds at most. Overbeating breaks the cream cheese down and the frosting will slacken and never recover.

## Tips

- Grate the carrots coarsely. Fine grating turns them to pulp and the cake goes wet.
- Toast the walnuts for eight minutes first. It is a small step with a disproportionate payoff.
- Frosted, it needs the fridge; bring it back to room temperature for half an hour before serving.
